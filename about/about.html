About Visuals


Visuals is an interactive learning platform built to help students and self-learners understand computer science, mathematics, and circuit concepts through real-time visualization.


Instead of relying solely on theory, Visuals focuses on seeing logic in motion — from data structures to signal behavior. The platform is designed for learners who understand best by building and experimenting.



About the Creator


I’m Hujaifa Rafi, a computer science student and developer interested in low-level systems, simulation, and visual computing. This project reflects my belief that learning happens best when abstract ideas become tangible.
