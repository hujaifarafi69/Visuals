About Visuals

Visuals is an interactive learning platform focused on making computer science, mathematics, and physics concepts easier to understand through real-time visual simulations.

Instead of memorizing formulas or abstract definitions, Visuals allows learners to see how data structures, logic, and circuits behave dynamically. The goal is to bridge the gap between theory and intuition.
